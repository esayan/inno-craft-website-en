Game Privacy Policies

Inno-Craft LLC · Last updated: November 6, 2026

Each game has its own policy

Inno-Craft LLC publishes three games, and they differ in what they do and do not collect. Rather than one combined document, each game has its own privacy policy and terms of use, linked below. Open the policy for the game you are playing.

None of our games requires an account, and none of them sends personal data to servers operated by Inno-Craft LLC. We operate no backend that stores player data.

Our games

Dungeon Adventure BloodEclipse

Roguelike RPG · 12+ · iOS and Android

Shows ads through Unity Ads and offers in-app purchases handled by Apple and Google. Those third parties may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data under their own policies; we collect nothing ourselves.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Kampus: Istanbul

Visual novel · 12+ · iOS and Android

Runs fully offline with no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. Your player name, choices and saves stay in local files on your own device and are never transmitted.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

HopBop: Dance & Learn English

Kids education · 4+ · iOS and Android
